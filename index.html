About the project

Cartoon chaos.
A horror twist.

BOB is a parody inspired by the look and feel of cartoons, reimagined with a horror edge. It takes the familiar, playful language of animation and bends it into something eerie, odd, and darkly funny—like a cartoon that wandered somewhere it really shouldn’t have.
